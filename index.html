How this is measured

A collector on a cloud server archives GRT's GTFS Realtime feeds every 30 seconds. Each trip update lists a predicted departure time for every stop a vehicle has not reached yet, and a stop drops off the list once the vehicle leaves it, so the last prediction before a stop disappears is taken as the actual departure and compared with the timetable.

A departure counts as on time between 1 minute early and 5 minutes late. First stops of each trip are left out, since a bus waiting at its terminal can drop off the feed before it leaves. Trips with no vehicle tracking and gaps in the archive are excluded rather than guessed.
